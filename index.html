About MTN Datahub

Reliable digital data bundles for customers in Ghana.

MTN Datahub is an online data bundle service that makes it simple to purchase MTN data plans from your phone or computer. We provide affordable, high-speed bundles with a straightforward checkout experience and fast processing.

What we offer

Our available plans range from starter and everyday bundles to larger value plans for heavy users. Each plan is clearly displayed with its price before payment, and customers provide the MTN number where the bundle should be delivered.

How it works

  • Select an available data bundle.
  • Enter the recipient's MTN number and a valid email address for the payment receipt.
  • Complete payment securely through Paystack.
  • Receive your data bundle and confirmation notification after successful processing.

Our commitment

We aim to provide clear pricing, secure payment processing, responsive order handling, and a convenient customer experience. For payment-related questions, customers should first keep their Paystack reference available when contacting us.
